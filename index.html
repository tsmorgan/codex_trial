Alcohol over an evening

Add drinks and update the graph to see an illustrative estimate of blood alcohol concentration over time.

1. Add drinks

Enter a whole hour, such as 4pm, 12am or 09:00. Drinks are assumed to be consumed over 45 minutes. Drag the ≡ handle to put drinks in chronological order, including after midnight, then select Update graph. A time earlier than the previous drink is treated as the following day. You can also focus a handle and use the up and down arrow keys.

    2. Estimated alcohol in the body

    The dark curve shows the combined estimated alcohol level after absorption and elimination. BAC means blood alcohol concentration, expressed in mg of alcohol per 100 mL of blood.

    Hover over the graph to inspect an estimated value at any time.

    Assumptions and limitations: An 80 kg adult, Widmark distribution factor 0.68, linear absorption over 45 minutes and BAC elimination of 15 mg/100 mL per hour. These are illustrative defaults, not personal measurements. The zero time is rounded to the nearest minute and is not an exact physiological prediction. Food, body composition, sex, medication, liver function and other factors can substantially change the result. Never use this estimate to decide whether to drive or perform safety-critical activities.
